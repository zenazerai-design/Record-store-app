Context

One platform. Three brands.

eApply is HCUK’s strategic platform for managing dealer leads and progressing vehicle finance applications across Genesis, Hyundai and Kia.

The challenge

Shipping features wasn’t enough.

eApply had been evolving for years, with new features being shipped to support dealers managing finance applications. But HCUK was under pressure to demonstrate the value of its investment in the platform, including the engineering effort and consultancy spend behind it.

I identified a gap: the team needed a clearer way to connect product development to measurable business outcomes and understand where the dealer experience could improve. Shipping features alone wasn’t enough to demonstrate impact.

Soft Search

Turning business objectives into product decisions.

Soft Search was an opportunity to use the finance journey to address the business goals identified during discovery: improving customer conversion, increasing acceptance rates for used cars, and giving dealers greater clarity without discouraging customers from proceeding.

These objectives helped frame what the feature needed to achieve before we defined the MVP scope.

Business objectives

Stakeholder framing from discovery: what the Soft Search work needed to move, before we locked MVP scope.

01

Increase customer conversion

02

Improve acceptance rates

Focus on acceptance rates for used cars in particular.

03

Provide dealers with clarity

Help dealers understand a customer’s indicative affordability without deterring them from proceeding with a proposal.

From discovery

Pain points dealers kept repeating.

Themes from calls and workshops: the friction on the forecourt behind the business case.

  1. Showroom hesitation

    Customers hesitant to apply for finance, resulting in missed sales opportunities.

  2. Negotiation dead-ends

    Time wasted “haggling” with customers whose applications were later rejected.

  3. Broken hand-offs

    Painful double-keying of customer information into multiple systems.

Journey

The friction was happening in the showroom.

Design

Making the results easier to understand.

Dealer feedback showed that results could feel too harsh and important information could be missed. I refined the hierarchy, introduced more reassuring cues and added an explanatory step.
